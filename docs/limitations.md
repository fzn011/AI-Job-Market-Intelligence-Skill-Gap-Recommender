# Project Limitations

- The default dataset is synthetic unless users import their own CSV data.
- Curated role profiles are simplified; role expectations vary by country, company, and seniority.
- Career category guidance is rule-based and transparent — not live hiring data.
- Rule-based skill extraction depends on dictionary/taxonomy quality and may miss nuanced phrasing.
- Cluster names are heuristic labels derived from top terms/skills, not official taxonomies.
- No paid LLM APIs are used; recommendations are deterministic and rule-based.
- On Streamlit Community Cloud, uploaded/generated files may be temporary.
- Outputs are educational decision-support tools, not hiring guarantees.
